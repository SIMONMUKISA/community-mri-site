Progress

Where things stand

We're building a low-cost, compact desktop MRI scanner from the ground up — and there's still a lot of room for people to join the project. The build is split into five phases, and we're laying the groundwork for Phase 1 right now.

What we've done so far

  • Launched this website
  • Secured studio space to build in
  • 3D printed a WOBOT toy robot prototype — we're selling these as a fundraiser toward a large-format 3D printer

The build, phase by phase

1
In progress

Building the magnet

Our desktop MRI uses cube-shaped permanent magnets — 4,800 neodymium magnets arranged in a Halbach array — which is what lets us keep the whole machine small. The magnet assembly is made up of 25 individual plates that hold the magnets in place to form the array.

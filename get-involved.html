Get involved

There are a few ways to help

We're a small, volunteer-powered project building a desktop MRI scanner from the ground up — pick whichever way of pitching in fits you best.

Donate

Proceeds from WOBOT and direct gifts go straight toward what Phase 1 needs most: a large-format 3D printer and the 4,800 magnets for our Halbach array.

Give today

Volunteer

We need hands for 3D printing, electronics, assembly, and outreach. No experience required to get started — just fill out the form below and we'll follow up.

Sign up to help

Partner with us

Two ways to partner: run a fundraiser for us outside of WOBOT sales (a drive, an event, a matching gift at your workplace), or lend technical support — engineering, electronics, RF, software, or manufacturing expertise for the build.
